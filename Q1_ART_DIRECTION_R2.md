# Quest 1 production art direction

The earlier chat is the creative brief. Q1-1 provides a minimum quality benchmark, not a requirement to repeat its textures. Aim for coherent, richly detailed woodland rooms with distinct compositions, stable lighting and clear gameplay footprints.

The new floor paintings contain only traversable ground. Blocking scenery is a separate alpha layer placed by game coordinates. Actors, health bars, labels, grids and controls remain runtime layers.

New artwork was produced with the built-in image generation tool. Technical exports preserve the generated composition and alpha while resizing and encoding to WebP for use in the game.

## Production prompt set

Shared direction: premium painted realistic fantasy woodland; high overhead viewpoint; warm upper-left daylight; cool green shade; natural moss, stone and leaf textures; no baked interface or gameplay labels.

1. Woodland Path: a gently meandering west-east path through low emerald moss, tiny white flowers and copper leaves. Entire rectangular floor is flat and traversable, without standing obstacles.
2. Training Clearing: broad worn sunlit earth and grass with subtle approach paths and richer low vegetation at its perimeter. A continuous floor, without walls or props.
3. Old Watchpost: weathered grey-green sandstone flagstones reclaimed by fine moss, leaf litter and low plants. A faint circular paving pattern gives the guardian area identity. No standing walls baked into the floor.
4. Moss boulders: a compact natural group of weathered grey boulders, velvety moss and delicate ferns; transparent background and readable square footprint.
5. Oak stump: hollow broken stump with detailed bark, tightly curling roots, moss and ferns; transparent exterior and compact footprint.
6. Vertical ruin: connected low sandstone wall running north-south, moss and tiny ivy, transparent exterior, suited to two vertically adjacent blocked cells.
7. Corner ruin: connected L-shaped wall along the right and bottom halves, with the upper-left quadrant transparent; clipped at runtime to three blocked cells.
8. D6: a warm ivory stone cube with six dark pips, ruby eyes, articulated stone legs and small moss/leaf details. Transparent background, no HP bars or scenery.
9. D8 guardian: an octahedral sandstone die with triangular faces, engraved 8, amber eyes, restrained bronze ornament and moss. Transparent background, no platform, aura ring or interface.

The images are individually usable production assets. No concept sheet or fabricated UI illustration is presented as an in-game result.
