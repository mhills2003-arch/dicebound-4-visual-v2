# Rushing Dunes artwork

Runtime files are WebP exports of eight generated paintings. Existing D6 and D8 sprites supply the ordinary guards. The Swift painting uses the Caravan Gate D6 as its visual reference.

## Generation prompts

### ground

`public/assets/premium-desert/rushing-dunes-ground-r1.webp`

Use case: stylized-concept. Production environment asset for DICEBOUND, a premium painterly tactical fantasy RPG. Warm late-afternoon sun from upper left, rim-catching soft gold, ochre sandstone, soft dusty apricot and shaded rust, quiet blue-violet bounce in the shadows. Beautiful hand-painted physical material detail inspired by a premium illustrated RPG. Near overhead orthographic camera angled only slightly toward the far side, no horizon, flat consistent plan view. Designed to read very clearly at tablet scale. Create one edge-to-edge landscape 13:9 painting containing ONLY traversable open sandy ground in Rushing Dunes. An airy wind-scoured desert basin beyond the caravan gate. Quiet shallow ripples in fine pale-gold sand and planar ivory-to-amber sweeps run diagonally across the broad board; intricate sgraffito-like wind traces, very small sparing embedded pebbles only. Spacious and legible from above. Broader, calmer sunwashed areas on the left and darker little terracotta streaks near the lower and right edges. Keep the centre lit, rich and quiet. No standing dunes, no significant raised edges, no cliffs, discrete rocks, walls, trails of paving, paths, structures, props, plants, oasis, creatures, UI, framing or grid. This is the walkable underpainting; freestanding ridges will be placed as separate layers.
